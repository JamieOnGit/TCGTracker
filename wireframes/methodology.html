Methodology

Last updated 1 Sep 2026 · Version 1.3 · Changelog

Formula

market_cap(card, grade) = psa_population(card, grade) × floor_price_aud(card, grade)

Each card is a single printing: English and Japanese versions are separate cards with separate populations and prices. Totals (set, game, site) are sums of card-level market caps for the selected grade.

Floor price definition & source priority

The floor is the lowest credible price at which a copy of that card in that grade can be bought now, else the most recent sale. Sources in priority order:

  1. ask — lowest active ask on BRAND marketplace or eBay AU (Buy It Now, AU-located, incl. postage), if ≥ 2 active asks and within guardrails.
  2. last sale — median of the last 3 completed sales in 30 days (BRAND sold listings, eBay AU sold).
  3. last sale (90d) — fallback window, flagged.

The basis tag shown next to every floor (“ask”, “last sale”) tells you which rule applied.

Outlier guardrails

  • Asks < 40% or > 250% of the 30-day median sale are ignored.
  • Sales flagged as best-offer-accepted without a visible price, bundles and “lots” are excluded.
  • Max daily floor move capped at ±25% unless confirmed by 2+ sales.
  • Excluded sales remain visible on card pages with an “excluded · outlier” tag.

Last-sale fallback

If no qualifying ask exists, we use last sale. With no sale in 90 days, the card shows “—” and is excluded from market cap totals (count shown as “untracked”).

Currency (FX)

All prices are AUD. Non-AUD sales are converted at the RBA daily rate (4pm AEST) on the sale date. Displayed prices include GST where the seller charged it.

RRP for drops

RRP comes from distributor price lists and retailer launch prices, reviewed by editors. AT RRP = within ±2%; BELOW/ABOVE show the % difference.

Data sources & attribution

  • Population: PSA public population reports / PSA API (© Collectors Universe).
  • Sales: BRAND marketplace; eBay AU completed listings.
  • Card catalogue: pokemontcg.io (Pokémon EN), official card lists (JP, One Piece), editor-mapped.
  • FX: Reserve Bank of Australia.
