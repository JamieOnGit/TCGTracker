FAQs

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Dashboard → Subscription (Stripe billing portal). You keep Premium until the end of the paid month; no partial refunds except where required by Australian Consumer Law.

How fast are drop alerts?

Premium alerts are sent as soon as our retailer monitors detect a change (typically within 1–3 minutes). The public drop history shows the same events 30+ minutes later.

Which retailers do you monitor?

Premium Bandai AU, JB Hi-Fi, EB Games, BIG W and Kmart. We add retailers over time; see the drops page for monitor status.

What happens to my listings if I downgrade?

Active listings stay live until they expire. New listings are limited to 5 per month again.

Is the price in AUD and does it include GST?

Yes — A$12.99 per month including GST. You’ll get a tax invoice by email.
